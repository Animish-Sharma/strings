# Survivorship

The archive is a survivorship-biased sample of a survivorship-biased sample.
What was written, of what happened. What survived, of what was written. What was
catalogued, of what survived. Every claim resting on the record inherits all
three filters.

## Absence claims inherit them fatally

Three different things produce the same silence:

1. nothing was written,
2. something was written and did not survive,
3. something survived and was never catalogued or has not been read.

A claim of absence that does not distinguish these is a claim about the archive
dressed as a claim about the past. The `survivorship` gate asks for the
distinguishing argument: **why would a record exist and survive, if the thing had
happened?**

That question is answerable. Certain record types were kept systematically, in
archives that survive, with known gaps. Where the answer is "it would have been
recorded here, that series survives complete for these years, and it is not
there", an absence claim is strong. Where the answer cannot be given, the honest
statement is about the record: *no surviving source mentions it*.

## Every claim needs a discriminator

Not only absence claims. The gate requires
`what_the_record_would_look_like_if_false`, and the reason is simple: if the
answer is "the same", the dossier's sources are *consistent with* the claim
rather than *evidence for* it.

That distinction is the audit. A dossier that cannot state it has assembled
material rather than tested anything.

## Record the coverage

`archive_coverage`: which archives were searched, which were not, and which are
known lost. An unrecorded search cannot be told apart from one nobody ran, and
the difference is exactly what an absence claim depends on.

## Do not generalize from what survived

The surviving record is not a sample of its period; it is a sample of what
institutions kept and fire spared. "Typical", "commonly", "throughout the period"
require an argument about survival, not a count of instances. The scope gate asks
for `systematic_sample` before it will let those words through.
