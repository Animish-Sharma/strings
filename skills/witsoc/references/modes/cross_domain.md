# Cross-domain work

Decompose a mixed target into domain-owned subclaims and one explicit synthesis
claim. Each subclaim keeps its own adapter, evidence ceiling, and vocabulary.
Exchange only bridge-v2 envelopes bound to the same frozen target, canonical
packet types, and a continuous causation chain.

Before transfer, state the source object, destination object, invariant being
preserved, assumptions introduced, and a falsifier. A useful analogy becomes a
candidate route, never evidence. A fatal objection from any engaged domain
blocks the synthesis claim. Conflicting outputs remain visible until an
Explorer decision cites evidence that resolves or scopes the conflict.

Identity transfer must be byte-identical. Any lossy conversion embeds its exact
source packet and declares semantic loss. Every hop preserves target, type,
content, scope ownership, and the intersection of named invariants. Refuse a
chain that exceeds its loss budget or changes scope without its owner.
