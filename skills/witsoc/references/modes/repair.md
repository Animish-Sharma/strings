# Repair

Repair may enter directly only with a frozen target, current artifact, failing
receipt or diagnostic, and an unchanged protected surface.

Classify the failure, state one repair hypothesis, mutate one axis, rerun the
cheapest discriminating check, and record the obligation delta. Repeated
failure with no reduced obligation returns control to Explorer or escalates the
named obstruction. Never repair by changing the target, suppressing a gate, or
reusing a receipt from earlier bytes.
